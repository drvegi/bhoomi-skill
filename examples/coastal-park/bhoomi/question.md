# The decision

Should the firm accept the anchor buyer's offer, and how should it fund the
two partners who want to exit without selling the whole park to one buyer?

## Options on the table
1. Accept the anchor offer at ₹1.35 Cr per acre (developed) and pay exits from sales.
2. Sell the anchor parcel raw (buyer develops inside its boundary) at a lower price.
3. Bring in an investor for a stake and use the money for exits.
4. Joint development with a developer's upfront deposit.

## Constraints
- Partner A wants cash within about 6 months.
- No sale of the restricted 4 acres until cleared.
- Partner C wants to keep running the park.
