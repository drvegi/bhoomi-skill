# Legal and regulatory checklist (India, land and industrial parks)

State-specific names are Andhra Pradesh examples; use the equivalent for the
deal's state. Verify current rules before relying on them.

## Title

- [ ] Chain of title for at least 30 years; link documents for every transfer
- [ ] Encumbrance certificate covering the same period
- [ ] Revenue records match (AP: Adangal, ROR-1B / webland; survey and
      sub-division numbers, extents, classification)
- [ ] Prohibited-property lists (AP: Section 22-A, Registration Act):
      every survey number checked; any listing and its resolution status
- [ ] Assigned, endowment, government or ceiling-surplus land excluded
- [ ] Litigation, attachments, mortgages and lis pendens searched
- [ ] Physical survey matches records; boundaries and encroachments

## Ownership and authority

- [ ] Owning entity identified (individuals, firm, company, LLP)
- [ ] Partnership deed / articles: who can sell, consent thresholds
- [ ] GPAs: validity, scope, registration, revocation risk
- [ ] Family or co-owner consents; minors' interests (court permission)

## Land use and conversion

- [ ] Land classification and conversion status (AP: NALA conversion);
      fee waivers are not the same as conversion orders
- [ ] Master plan / zoning designation for the proposed use
- [ ] Layout approval: approved plan, conditions, plots mortgaged to the
      authority until works are complete, amendment process for changes

## Park or scheme approvals

- [ ] Government order and its conditions (eligibility of buyers, sectors,
      minimum infrastructure, timelines, transfer restrictions, claw-backs)
- [ ] Incentives tied to milestones and what breaks them
- [ ] Whether approvals transfer with a sale of the whole, or of plots
- [ ] Commitments by government departments (roads, power, water): status

## Environment and safety

- [ ] Coastal Regulation Zone (2019 notification): category, No Development
      Zone, approved coastal zone management plan map, permitted uses
- [ ] Industrial estate environmental clearance thresholds
- [ ] Pollution board consent to establish / operate; green-belt rules by
      unit category (MoEFCC office memorandum of October 2025)
- [ ] Buffers: high-tension lines, water bodies, neighbouring hazardous units
- [ ] Fire and disaster-management requirements for the layout

## Transaction documents

- [ ] MoUs, exclusivity, advances and their expiry; claims; releases needed
- [ ] JDA or sale terms: obligations, milestones, defaults, step-in rights
- [ ] Covenants that must run with the land (green strips, maintenance,
      park rules) and how they are enforced
- [ ] RERA: whether the plotted scheme falls under the state RERA rules
- [ ] FEMA for NRI buyers (agricultural land restriction)
- [ ] Stamp duty and registration for each document

## Disclosure

- [ ] Every open item above that a buyer's due diligence will find is
      disclosed in the offer documents
