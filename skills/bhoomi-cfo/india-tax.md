# India tax and duty checklist for land deals

A map of what to check, not advice. **Verify every rate, threshold and date
against the current Income-tax Act, Finance Act and state stamp schedule
before use** (web search; cite the source). A chartered accountant must
confirm the position.

## Is the land a capital asset at all?

- **Section 2(14)(iii)**: agricultural land outside municipal or cantonment
  limits beyond prescribed aerial distances (graded by the municipality's
  population) is not a capital asset, so no capital gains tax. Evidence:
  revenue records showing agricultural use, a Tahsildar/RDO distance
  certificate, census population of the nearest municipality, and how the
  land is carried in the accounts.
- Conversion to non-agricultural use, or developing and selling plots, can
  weaken this position.

## Capital asset or stock-in-trade?

- **Section 45(2)**: converting a capital asset into stock-in-trade (for
  example by developing and selling plots as a business) splits the gain:
  capital gain up to conversion, business income after.
- Frequency of sales, plotting, advertising and development work are the
  usual indicators the tax department looks at.

## Sale of land or plots

- Long-term capital gains rate and indexation rules changed in July 2024;
  check what applies to the seller's status (individual, HUF, firm, company)
  and acquisition date.
- **Section 50C**: the sub-registrar guideline value is deemed the sale
  consideration if higher than the agreed price (subject to the tolerance
  band in the Act).
- **Section 194-IA**: buyer deducts TDS on immovable property above the
  threshold; rural agricultural land is excluded.
- **Section 54B / 54F / 54EC**: check exemptions for reinvestment where the
  seller is eligible.

## Firms, partners and exits

- **Section 45(4)** and **Section 9B** (from Finance Act 2021): money or
  assets paid to a partner on reconstitution or retirement can be taxed in
  the firm's hands.
- Sale of a partner's interest vs the firm selling land: different tax and
  stamp-duty outcomes.
- Conversion of a firm to a company or LLP: check the conditions for
  tax-neutral conversion.

## Joint development

- **Section 45(5A)**: for individuals and HUFs, capital gains on a
  registered JDA arise when the completion certificate is issued.
- **Section 2(47)(v)**: handing over possession under a JDA can be a
  transfer.

## GST

- Sale of land is outside GST (Schedule III). Development services,
  JDA arrangements and sale of developed plots raise questions:
  check current CBIC clarifications and rulings.

## Stamp duty and registration

- State-specific. In Andhra Pradesh, conveyances attract stamp duty,
  transfer duty and a registration fee; check the current schedule.
- Leases, development agreements, GPAs and partnership deeds each carry
  their own duty.
- Payment in plots (in-kind) attracts duty on each transfer.

## NRI buyers

- FEMA bars NRIs from buying agricultural land, plantations and farmhouses.
  Check the land's classification at the time of sale.
